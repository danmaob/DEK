# Estimation & Prioritization

## Estimation
Use relative sizing (S/M/L or story points) over false-precision hour
estimates for anything not already well understood. Base estimates on
the acceptance criteria, not the story title — a story with five
criteria is not the same size as one with two.

State explicitly what could make an estimate wrong (an unknown
dependency, an unclear integration point) rather than padding the
number silently.

## Prioritization
Default to a simple value-vs-effort view, or MoSCoW
(Must/Should/Could/Won't) when stakeholders need clearer language.
Prioritize by:
1. Blocking dependencies (does other work wait on this?).
2. Business value stated in the requirements.
3. Risk reduction (does this de-risk a bigger unknown?).
4. Effort, as the tie-breaker, not the primary factor.

## Anti-patterns
- Re-prioritizing every session without a stated reason.
- Estimating a story that has no acceptance criteria yet.
